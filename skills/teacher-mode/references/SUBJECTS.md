## 1. PHYSICS (Physics VI + VII)

**Syllabus (CBSE 2024-25):**
- Electrostatics (Capacitance, Dielectrics, Parallel/C-Series)
- Current Electricity (Kirchhoff, Wheatstone, Potentiometer, Kamm)
- Magnetism & Materials (Biot-Savart, Ampere, Diamag/Para/Ferromag)
- EMI & AC (Self/L-Inductance, RLC, Transformer, AC Meters)
- E & M Waves (Maxwell, Spectrum)
- Optics (Refraction, Lenses, Microscope/Telescope, Diffraction, Polarization)
- Modern Physics (Photoelectric, Wave-Particle, Atoms, Semiconductors)

**High-weight chapters:** Modern Physics, Optics, EMI-AC (50-60% of marks)

**Frequently asked (PYQ):**
- Photoelectric equation numericals
- De Broglie wavelength
- Derivation of Bohr orbits
- Diffraction minima formula
- Transformer ratio

**Common mistakes:**
- Confusing capacitance in series/parallel formulas
- Sign errors in Kirchhoff's
- Missing units in numericals

# STUDY STRATEGY

**Weekly prep plan:**
- Mon: Physics numericals + Math proofs
- Tue: Chemistry name reactions + Bio diagrams
- Wed: English writing practice + Hindi composition
- Thu: PYQ practice all subjects
- Fri: Full syllabus revision
- Sat: Mock test (3 hr)
- Sun: Weak areas revision

**Exam day tips:**
- 15 min for reading paper
- Answer easy questions first
- Time per mark: 1.5 min
- Leave 30 min for review

**Subject priority:**
1. High-weight chapters first (50% marks from 40% content)
2. PYQ practice daily
3. Diagrams/derivations separately
4. Numericals practice with timing
5. Answer in full sentences (CBSE awards for explanations)
